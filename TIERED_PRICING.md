# Busara AI — Tiered Pricing Structure

Per the Brutally Honest Review:
"Structure at least three tiers: a self-serve tier priced in that $15–40/seat
band to drive volume and word-of-mouth, a team tier with governance/connectors,
and a custom enterprise tier (SSO, audit log, dedicated connectors, SLAs)."

## Pricing Tiers

### 1. Starter (Self-Serve) — $29/seat/month
- Up to 10 analyses/month
- 50 AI agents, all 7 stages
- CSV/JSON upload + African connectors (M-Pesa, Flutterwave, Paystack)
- Export results (JSON, CSV, PDF)
- 7-day analysis history
- Email support
- Target: individual analysts, small SMBs, solo founders

### 2. Professional (Team) — $99/seat/month
- Unlimited analyses
- 50 AI agents, all 7 stages
- All connectors + scheduled reports
- Team collaboration (shared workspace)
- Business memory (persistent metric definitions, corrections)
- 90-day audit trail + RBAC
- Show-the-work transparency (SQL/Python trail)
- WhatsApp/Slack digest delivery
- Priority support (24h response)
- Target: data teams, growing SMEs, consultancies

### 3. Enterprise — Custom ($25K–$100K+/year)
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- SSO (SAML, Google Workspace, Azure AD)
- Dedicated connectors (custom data sources)
- On-premise / VPC deployment option
- SLA (99.9% uptime, 4-hour incident response)
- Custom agent fine-tuning
- Dedicated success manager
- Quarterly business reviews
- Compliance (SOC 2, GDPR, Kenya Data Protection Act)
- Target: banks, SACCOs, government agencies, large enterprises

## Payment Methods

### African Customers (Starter & Professional)
- **Flutterwave**: M-Pesa, cards, bank transfer (KES, NGN, GHS, ZAR)
- **Paystack**: Cards, bank transfer (NGN, GHS, ZAR)

### International Customers (All tiers)
- **Stripe**: Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay (USD, EUR, GBP)
- **Paddle**: Merchant of Record (handles VAT/sales tax compliance for EU/US)

### Enterprise
- Invoice/PO (annual billing)
- Bank transfer (wire/ACH)
- Flutterwave/Stripe for monthly billing

## Free Trial
- 14-day full-access trial (Professional tier)
- No credit card required
- Convert to paid at any time during trial

## Notes
- All prices in USD; local currency billing available via Flutterwave/Paystack
- Annual billing: 2 months free (pay for 10 months)
- Non-profit/NGO discount: 30% off all tiers
- Startup discount: 50% off Professional for first year (YC, local accelerators)
